The first useful version

Simple enough to launch. Careful enough to protect the network.

The early workflow is intentionally manual: gather the event details, qualify the request, route it to fitting artisans, and coordinate clearly before we automate anything.

  1. 01Tell us the occasion

    Event type, timing, location, guest count, budget range, and services needed.

  2. 02We match the request

    Requests are routed only when they fit the right category, service area, and scope.

  3. 03Artists quote with context

    Vendors get cleaner details, fewer dead-end messages, and better-fit opportunities.
